Listening Atlas: three directions

The Overview page redrawn three ways on real data. Pick one, or name the pieces to combine. The trade-offs are in web/DESIGN.md.

  • A. Liner Notes

    Editorial. One sentence about your listening, your top artist's portrait, album covers in a record-shop grid.

  • B. Sleeve

    Immersive. The page takes its colour from the sleeve you pick; choose another track and everything re-tints.

  • C. Groove

    A data instrument. A vinyl record whose grooves are years and whose angle is the hour of the day.
